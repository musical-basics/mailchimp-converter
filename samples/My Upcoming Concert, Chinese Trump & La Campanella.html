Third - and this one is interesting for those of you who love practicing piano…


What happens when you play La Campanella on a “Narrower Keyboard”? (I just released this video 30 minutes ago. Not even joking.)


In this video, I compared what it’s like to play La Campanella on a standard keyboard vs. the narrower DS6.0 keyboard.


Not theory. Not opinions. A real, physical demonstration.


On a standard keyboard, small hands pay a hidden tax on:

1) Every big jump

2) Every wide chord

3) Every octave run

More tension. More practice hours. More risk.

You can watch the video here:

La Campanella Comparison Video

Same notes. Same music.
But less distance to travel - which means less strain and more control.
